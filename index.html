How to Use Blackjack Pro

Follow these steps for every hand to maintain an accurate count:

  • Initialize the Shoe: Before you start, select the correct number of decks being used at your table from the Decks in Shoe dropdown. Click RESET SHOE to ensure the count starts at zero.
  • Inputting Cards: When prompted to enter cards, use standard abbreviations: 2–9 for numbered cards, 10 for tens, J, Q, K for face cards, and A for Aces. Separate each card with a space (e.g., K 7 A).
  • Getting Advice: If you are unsure of the best move, type your hand into the player input box and click the "?" button to see the suggested action (Hit or Stand).
  • Logging the Round: Once the round is over and all cards (yours and the dealer's) are revealed, type them into their respective fields. Click LOG ROUND. This calculates the current Running Count (RC) and True Count (TC) based on the cards removed from the deck.
  • Betting: Keep an eye on the Next Bet display. As the True Count rises, the system will adjust the suggested bet based on your Base Bet setting.
  • Insurance: Check the INSURANCE status in the side panel. If it says "YES", the count is high enough (TC ≥ 3) that taking insurance is statistically favored.
